# IZ4 trademarks: the short version

**Open to fork. Protected to mean something.**

The IZ4 format is open, and the iz4 CLI is open source under the Apache
License 2.0. You can use, study, change and fork them without asking. The
IZ4 name and badge belong to Nige Ltd, and they are protected for one
reason: so that "IZ4" keeps meaning something.

**If it carries the IZ4 name, the Foundation remains.**

## You may, without asking

- Talk and write about IZ4, link to it, and compare things with it.
- Say truthfully that something works with IZ4, reads IZ4 files or uses
  the iz4 CLI.
- Use, change and redistribute the software under its licence.
- Call your project's file an IZ4 when it is one.
- Fork the code, the format or the protocol, and take it anywhere,
  under a name of your own.

## You may, once your project is registered

Register a project on iz4.you and it gets the
[IZ4 Trademark Licence](https://iz4.you/legal/trademark-licence), the same for everyone.
While it conforms, you may:

- call it an IZ4 project and use the IZ4 name for it;
- show the IZ4 badge the register issues for it;
- say which IZ4 versions it conforms to.

## Please don't

- Suggest an unrelated project is a registered or official IZ4 project.
- Show the badge without a registration and an active licence, or on a
  project or version it was not issued for.
- Call a fork that changed the Foundation "IZ4", or present changed
  Foundation text as official.
- Suggest that IZ4 certifies, audits, endorses or guarantees anything.
- Redraw, recolour or combine the marks, or use them as your own name,
  logo or domain.

## Forks are welcome

Open source gives you the freedom to change it. The IZ4 mark tells people
what you chose not to change. Change the code, the stack, even the
protocol. Keep calling it IZ4? Keep the Foundation. Change the
Foundation? Then it is your protocol, so give it your own name.

## If something is off

The marks protect a shared meaning. If a use of them no longer matches
that meaning, we will normally just ask for it to be corrected.
